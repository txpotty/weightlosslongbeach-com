    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Lakewood, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Lakewood

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT protects follicles from the slow miniaturization behind pattern loss. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Lakewood

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. Around month six, density starts to improve. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Lakewood

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. Most of these fade after stopping, and mood changes need prompt reporting. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Lakewood

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
