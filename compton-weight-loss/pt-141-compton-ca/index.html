    PT-141 activates melanocortin receptors in the brain, mainly MC4, which play a part in sexual motivation and arousal signaling rather than blood flow to the genitals. Sildenafil and tadalafil work on blood vessels, so PT-141 is a different tool. It targets wanting, not plumbing. Vyleesi is approved for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized hypoactive sexual desire disorder.

    Dosing and Schedule in Compton

    Brand Vyleesi is a 1.75 mg autoinjector used in the belly or thigh at least 45 minutes before anticipated sexual activity, and only when needed rather than on a daily schedule.
