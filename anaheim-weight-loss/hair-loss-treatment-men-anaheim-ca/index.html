    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Anaheim, CA

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Anaheim

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil, on its own path, helps follicles grow longer and thicker.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. Results are strongest where some hair remains, since long-bald areas seldom regrow. Earlier is better. Patchy bald spots, scalp pain or rapid loss call for a dermatologist visit. The provider checks your photos for the typical pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Anaheim

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label daily pill some providers prescribe instead, typically starting around 1.25 to 2.5 mg. Compounded sprays that combine finasteride and minoxidil go on once daily. Dutasteride is a capsule taken daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. Around months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening. Fuller results show between nine and twelve months.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Anaheim

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. They also ask directly about past depression, current sexual health and any plans to have children soon, since those shape which medicine is chosen. Finasteride lowers PSA results by about half. Mention it at your next prostate screening.

    How Men's Options Compare in Anaheim

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Men avoiding DHT blockers can use a compounded minoxidil and GHK-Cu topical. Surgery is an option for some.
