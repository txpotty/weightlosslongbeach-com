    How the Energy Booster Works in Garden Grove

    The Energy Booster is a compounded formula built around nutrients involved in how the body makes and uses energy, especially B vitamins like B12. Low B12 leads to fewer healthy red blood cells, which can leave you tired and foggy. Deficiency matters most. When levels are already normal, extra B vitamins usually make a smaller difference.     Who the Energy Booster Is For

    Adults who feel tired most days despite seven or more hours of sleep, and who want a provider-guided approach rather than store-bought energy drinks, are the usual fit. People on metformin or long-term acid reducers, vegans and older adults are more likely to run low on B12. It has limits. It is not a treatment for sleep disorders, depression or thyroid disease. Sudden severe fatigue needs in-person care.
